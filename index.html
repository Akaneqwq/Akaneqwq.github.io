Publications

First-author Publications

Pair2Point: Distilling Pairwise Reasoning into Pointwise Rewards for High-Quality Text-to-Image Generation

Qian Wang, Xinhang Leng, Yinan Li, Sujie Hu, Huaisong Zhang, Chen Li, Jian Zhang

AAAI, 2027 (under review)

We propose Pair2Point, a two-stage training paradigm for text-to-image reward models. It first trains a pairwise reasoning model and then distills it into an efficient pointwise scoring model, addressing the low efficiency of pairwise comparisons and the instability of pointwise scoring. We also construct a corresponding benchmark, a preference dataset, and a pointwise dataset.

AgentKE: Agentic Knowledge Editing for Diffusion Transformers

Qian Wang, Shuyu Wang, Zhile Guan, Xinhua Cheng, Xiandong Meng, Jian Zhang

AAAI, 2027 (under review)

We propose AgentKE, a knowledge editing framework for text-to-image diffusion transformers. It leverages a VLM to decompose natural language intents and generate corresponding protection sets, then edits the parameters of linear projection layers in the null space of the protection sets to update or modify knowledge, with automatic reflection to improve the editing results.

DualDiff3D: Dual Structure-Appearance Diffusion Priors for Reliability-Enhanced 3D Gaussian Splatting

Qian Wang, Yu Wang, Weiqi Li, Xinhua Cheng, Xiandong Meng, Ronggang Wang, Jian Zhang

ECCV, 2026

We propose DualDiff3D, which employs two diffusion models to extract structure priors from low-quality rendered novel views and appearance priors from reference views, together with a reliability-enhanced Render-Refine-Optimize loop to improve 3D Gaussian Splatting reconstruction and novel view rendering from sparse views.

360DVD: Controllable Panorama Video Generation with 360-Degree Video Diffusion Model

Qian Wang, Weiqi Li, Chong Mou, Xinhua Cheng, Jian Zhang

CVPR, 2024

Paper Project Code

We propose a controllable panorama video generation pipeline named 360DVD for generating panoramic videos based on the prompts and motion conditions.
